What it can include

A clear list of knowns, gaps and next steps.

  • ✓Initial consultation to understand the property and intended transaction.
  • ✓Agreed checklist of documents and questions to investigate.
  • ✓Coordination of available records or referrals to relevant professionals.
  • ✓Written summary of items reviewed, gaps identified and suggested next actions.
  • ✓Optional site visit or additional professional checks, if separately agreed.

Good fit for

  • Buyers who want to organise their pre-purchase questions.
  • Owners preparing to sell, lease or renovate.
  • Families coordinating property matters from another city or country.
  • Owners unsure which professional to consult next.
The report is a coordination and information service, not a title certificate, legal opinion, structural certification or guarantee of approvals. Scope and fee are confirmed before work begins.
