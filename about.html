Our purpose

Make property work easier to navigate.

Property decisions often involve documents, contractors, agents, budgets and timelines. Our intended role is to help customers understand the requirement, clarify the scope, connect with relevant professionals and keep agreed next steps organised.

We are starting with Mangaluru and will consider wider service areas based on reliable local partners and customer demand.

Our working principles

  • ✓Clarity: explain scope and charges before work starts.
  • ✓Transparency: distinguish estimates, vendor costs and statutory fees.
  • ✓Consent: share customer information only as authorised.
  • ✓Accountability: document decisions, updates and handovers.
Important: REALTORS 888 is not a government department and does not guarantee property approvals, legal outcomes, investment returns or third-party performance. Services and partner availability must be confirmed in writing.
