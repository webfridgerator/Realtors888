The REALTORS 888 approach

Less confusion. More informed decisions.

  • ✓Written scope: know what is and isn't included.
  • ✓Cost clarity: separate our fees from partner quotations and statutory charges.
  • ✓Documented updates: keep important decisions and agreed milestones visible.
  • ✓Appropriate professionals: specialist work is referred to suitably qualified partners.
